# C-3PO pet artwork

The C-3PO Codex pet is not bundled yet. This directory holds the manifest only,
so the installer, validation, and release packaging are ready for it.

Until the art ships, `agent-starwars pet install c3po` reports that the pet is
not bundled and changes nothing. Codex's native pet feature can create a pet in
the meantime.

When the art lands, add `spritesheet.webp` (8 x 11 atlas of 192 x 208 cells,
Codex sprite version 2), `LICENSE.txt` for the artwork, and its provenance here.
Validation and packaging enable themselves once the sprite sheet exists. The
art must be original: no frames, stills, or models extracted from Star Wars
films or games.
