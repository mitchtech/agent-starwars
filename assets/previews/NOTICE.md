# Preview images

[`scripts/build-assets.py`](../../scripts/build-assets.py) from the committed
clips in [`audio/`](../../audio). The droids in them are simple original
shapes drawn by that script, not frames, stills, or models from any Star Wars
film or game. The waveforms depict Star Wars audio, whose rights remain with
Lucasfilm Ltd.; see the [audio rights notice](../../audio/NOTICE.md).
